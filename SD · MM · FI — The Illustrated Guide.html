The Big Picture

SAP is built like a company: every department has a module, and the modules constantly hand each other paperwork. Three of them do the heavy lifting for money and goods. SD sells things, MM buys and stores things, and FI keeps the books.

When these three are wired together you get four gifts: real-time visibility (stock, sales and money update instantly), automation (one transaction triggers the next), auditability (everything is traceable) and agility (one version of the truth for decisions).

📖 Our running story. Global Bike Inc. sells 7 bicycles to The Bike Zone (Chapter 2), and a manufacturing unit buys 100 units of raw material (Chapter 3). Watch how goods and money move in step.

Order-to-Cash: Selling a Bike

O2C is the journey from “Can you quote me a price?” to “Thanks, the money has arrived.” Follow the dashed road below — each stop is coloured by the module in charge, and the small print shows the ledger entries it creates.

💡 Only two steps touch the books in a physical flow: goods issue (stock leaves, cost is booked) and billing (revenue is booked). Quotation and order carry no accounting entries at all.

Movement 601 & VKOA in one sentence each

Delivery triggers goods movement type 601 in MM, reducing stock and creating a material document. Billing uses account determination VKOA to map pricing conditions (taxes, discounts, surcharges) to the right G/L accounts.

Chapter 3

03

Procure-to-Pay: Buying Materials

P2P is O2C’s mirror image: instead of giving goods to a customer and waiting for cash, you receive goods from a vendor and eventually pay cash. Here MM leads and FI follows.

🔑 Three-way match: purchase order, goods receipt and vendor invoice must agree. Price or quantity differences are flagged for resolution. OBYC picks the G/L accounts automatically from valuation class and movement type.

Your ABAP Toolbox

Standard SAP does the plumbing. Your job is to add the company-specific rules — safely. These are the six main entry points.

🔌User ExitsPredefined hooks inside standard programs, e.g. MV45AFZZ (sales orders) and RV60AFZZ (billing).
🧩Customer ExitsFunction-module based extension points — add logic without touching standard code.
🧱BADIsObject-oriented, multiple implementations allowed. E.g. SD_SLS_MODIFY_HEAD_BUSINESS for order headers.
📞BAPIStandard function modules to create/change/read business objects, e.g. BAPI_SALESORDER_CREATEFROMDAT2. Custom fields via EXTENSIONIN.
✉️IDoc & ALEStructured, asynchronous messages between systems; ALE is the framework that distributes them.
⚡RFCSynchronous calls between SAP systems or with external applications.

Where they usually bite

Custom account determination: extend KOMPCV/KOMV and use EXIT_SAPLV60B_001 for special revenue or COGS mapping. Document flow: exits can attach custom attributes or external documents for traceability. Error handling: catch missing account determination or blocked invoices before they hit FI.

05

When Things Break

Most integration incidents fall into three families. Learn the symptom, and you already know where to look.

🚫Account determination failsGoods receipt or billing stops. Check valuation class, transaction key and account assignment group in OBYC (MM-FI) or VKOA (SD-FI).
🕸️Document flow is brokenStatuses and reports go wrong. Use correction reports such as SDVBUK00, and exits to repair the flow.
⚖️GR/IR won’t clearGoods and invoices disagree. Reconcile the open items regularly.
🧰 Detective kit: VFX3 (release billing to accounting) · FB03 (display accounting doc) · FBL5N / FBL1N (customer / vendor line items) · SM37 (background jobs) · MB51 (goods movements) · FS10N (G/L balances).

The S/4HANA Difference

In classic ECC the same business event was copied into many tables that had to be reconciled. S/4HANA writes it once, into the Universal Journal.

Advanced ATP (aATP)

The availability check grows up: product allocation, backorder processing and supply protection, evaluated at sales-order creation, so scarce stock goes to the right customers and nothing is oversold. In S/4HANA, Business Partner (BP) also replaces separate customer and vendor masters.

Five things to remember

① O2C and P2P tie logistics to finance at every step. ② Master data, account determination and movement types decide whether integration works. ③ User exits, BADIs, BAPI, IDoc and RFC are your levers. ④ Test both flows end to end, and test bad master data too. ⑤ In S/4HANA, ACDOCA and aATP simplify the picture.
